About Zoe Picks

Short, honest buying guides — and how we keep them honest.

What Zoe Picks is

Zoe Picks is an independent buying-guide website. We help shoppers choose between products in everyday categories — laptops, phones, monitors, TVs, kitchen appliances, fitness gear, and more — by putting the most relevant options side by side with their strengths, trade-offs, and who each one is best for.

How we pick

  • Start from the need. Every guide begins with a budget and a use case, then looks at the specs and features that actually matter for that use.
  • Compare real trade-offs. Each product lists pros and cons. There is rarely a single “best” for everyone, so we say who each pick suits.
  • Skip what we can’t verify. If a spec or price isn’t reliable, we leave it out or mark it as unverified instead of guessing.
  • Keep it current. Guides carry a date, and offers that expire are marked as ended or removed.

What our guides are (and are not)

Our guides are research-based comparisons built from manufacturer specifications, Amazon listing information, and publicly available buyer feedback. We do not claim to have run hands-on lab tests on every product. Prices, availability, and specifications change often, so please confirm the details on the retailer’s page before you buy.

How the site makes money

Zoe Picks is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. When you click a product link and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We may also show advertising. Commissions and ads never decide which products we include or how we describe them. Read the full Affiliate Disclosure.

Corrections and feedback

Found an error, an outdated price, or a product we should cover? Tell us on the Contact page. We review corrections and update guides as quickly as we can.
