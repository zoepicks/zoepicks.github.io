How to choose

  1. Decide what matters most. If you want the most silence, buy a Bose QuietComfort Ultra. If you want the best sound, look at the B&W PX7 S3 or the Sony.
  2. Check the battery. Both outlets say around 30 hours is the norm for flagships. The Marshall and 1More last far longer, while the Bose Ultra first gen and Bose QuietComfort manage 24 hours.
  3. Look for sales. TechRadar says the Bose QuietComfort Ultra often drops to about $300 and the Bose QuietComfort to about $200, so avoid paying full list price.
  4. Protect your hearing. TechRadar's audiologist says ANC lets you listen at a lower volume. Keep it to about 60 percent volume for no more than 60 minutes at a time.

FAQ

Which noise-cancelling headphones are best overall?

Tom's Guide picks the Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones (2nd Gen) and TechRadar scored them joint best for noise cancelling in its tests.

What is the best cheap pair?

TechRadar's best under $100 is the 1More SonoFlow Pro HQ51. Tom's Guide's best under $100 is the JLab JBuds Lux ANC, a $79 list-price pair with up to 40 hours of ANC battery.

Are the Sonos Ace Ultra worth considering?

Tom's Guide names the Sonos Ace Ultra its best alternative. Its ANC is a big upgrade, solidly above Sony and only slightly behind Bose, but at $449 it mainly makes sense if you own Sonos speakers.

Do noise-cancelling headphones replace hearing protection?

No. TechRadar's audiologist says ANC headphones are not hearing protection and are no substitute at concerts, shooting ranges or around power tools.
