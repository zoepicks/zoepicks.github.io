How to spot a real phone deal

  1. Compare with the brand's own list price, not the retailer's crossed-out price. Some stores still show pre-increase prices as the original.
  2. Look at the final price, not just the dollars off. Samsung raised Galaxy S26 prices by $100 or more in early October, so a big discount now can be the same real price as last year.
  3. Be careful with free phones from carriers. Carrier deals usually need a trade-in and a pricier plan, and the savings come as monthly credits over up to 36 months. If you leave early, you owe the balance.
  4. Check the model year and storage. Names can look almost identical across years, and the sale price is often for the smallest storage size.
  5. Check the return window and whether the phone is the US unlocked version before you buy.

FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30.

Should I buy now or wait for Black Friday?

Memeburn found that some phone prices in October's Prime sale already matched last year's Black Friday depth, for example the Galaxy S26 Ultra at $979. It also says foldables are where waiting looks smartest. If a phone you want is at a price you like, buying now is reasonable.

Is it better to buy unlocked or from a carrier?

Unlocked phones cost more upfront but let you switch carriers any time. Carrier deals look bigger but usually need a trade-in, a higher-priced plan and up to 36 months of bill credits.
