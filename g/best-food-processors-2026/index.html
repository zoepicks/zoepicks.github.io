How to choose

  1. Pick the right capacity. America's Test Kitchen thinks 11 cups or more is the most useful size for most home cooks, since it can chop, slice and knead small and large amounts.
  2. Check the noise. Tom's Guide measured 65.2 dB on the Cuisinart 14 Cup and 93.9 dB on the Hamilton Beach, so budget models can be much louder.
  3. You rarely need every accessory. America's Test Kitchen says most tasks need only chopping, slicing and shredding blades, and extra attachments add cost, storage and cleaning.
  4. Look for a responsive pulse button. America's Test Kitchen says a short, quick pulse gives you control over chop size and avoids over-processing.
