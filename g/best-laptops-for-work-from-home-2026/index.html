4 mistakes when buying a work-from-home laptop

  1. Chasing the processor. Sources stress that webcam quality, RAM and a comfortable keyboard matter more than the headline processor for home-office work.
  2. Skipping the webcam and RAM check on a budget laptop. The IdeaPad Slim picks are affordable, but the sources say to check the webcam and RAM before you buy.
