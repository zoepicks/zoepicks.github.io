How to choose

  1. Pick a size that fits your desk. PCWorld calls 27 inches the goldilocks size for most people, while a 24-inch screen suits a shallow desk and 32-inch or ultrawide screens suit deep desks.
  2. Choose USB-C if you use a laptop. PCWorld recommends USB-C for most people because one cable carries video and charges the laptop. Check the Power Delivery wattage against your laptop.
  3. Brightness matters more than HDR. PCWorld says 300 nits or more is ideal for a bright home office and that a home office monitor does not need HDR.
  4. 1080p is still fine. PCWorld says 1080p is more than adequate for most home office tasks, so you can save money unless you want sharper text.
