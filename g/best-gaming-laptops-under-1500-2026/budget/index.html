4 mistakes when buying a gaming laptop

  1. Expecting long battery life while gaming. A review roundup measured under 2 hours of gaming on battery for the ROG Strix G16. The Nitro V 16 gives about 3 hours of casual use.
  2. Ignoring the configuration. Some Nitro V 15 configs ship with 8GB of RAM, and GeekChamp's best LOQ value is only the discounted RTX 4060 versions.
  3. Skipping the resolution check. Most picks here are named for 1080p gaming. Eneba names the Nitro V 16 for 1080p, and the LOQ 15 is good for 1080p.
  4. Forgetting heat and weight. The ROG Strix G16 can run hot under heavy load and is slightly heavy, and the TUF Gaming A15 / A16 is heavier than some rivals.
