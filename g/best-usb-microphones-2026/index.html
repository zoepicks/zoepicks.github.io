How to choose

  1. Match the pattern to your use. Cardioid is best when you record alone. Choose a mic with omnidirectional or bidirectional modes if you record with guests.
  2. Check the connection. The Shure MV7+ and MV7i also have XLR, which lets you move to a studio setup later. Plain USB mics like the JLab Talk are simpler.
  3. Plan for a pop filter and stand. The Cherry Ngale R has no muff, and the Shure MV7i comes with no stand or boom arm, so budget a little extra.
  4. Use the app tools. Shure's Motiv Mix denoiser removed cafe noise in Tom's Guide's test, which can save a lot of editing time.
