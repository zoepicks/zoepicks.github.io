How to spot a real laptop deal

  1. Compare with the lowest recent price, not the crossed-out list price. Memeburn warns that Black Friday discounts often look bigger than they are.
  2. Match the exact configuration. RAM, storage, screen type and graphics power can differ between two listings with the same name, as the LOQ and OmniBook above show.
  3. Aim for 16GB of memory on Windows laptops. 8GB is workable on a MacBook Neo for light use, but reviewers call it a real limit on Windows.
  4. Move fast on good deals. GamesRadar warned that some early gaming deals could sell out quickly.
  5. Check the return window before you buy, especially for a laptop you cannot test in a store.

FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30.

Should I buy now or wait for Black Friday?

TechRadar notes retailers may be holding back some of their best laptop deals until Black Friday. GamesRadar found some early deals already near typical Black Friday prices. If a laptop you want is already at a price you are happy with, buying now is reasonable.

Is the Amazon price the same as Best Buy?

Not always. Several deals above were reported at Best Buy or Apple. Use the Amazon button to compare the current price and the exact configuration.

Is 8GB of memory enough?

For light browsing and schoolwork on the MacBook Neo, reviewers say yes. For heavy multitasking or Windows laptops, 16GB is the safer choice.
