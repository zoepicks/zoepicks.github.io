4 mistakes students make when buying a laptop

  1. Trusting the battery number on the box. Battery figures here come from PCWorld's tests, and they depend on use. The Surface Pro lasts up to about 19 hours in light use but only about 2 hours at full brightness under heavy use.
  2. Picking the wrong operating system. Check your course software first. A Chromebook runs ChromeOS only, so Windows or Mac programs will not install.
  3. Ignoring the exact configuration. Specs here are for the version PCWorld tested. Amazon listings can differ in processor, RAM and storage, so check the listing before you buy.
  4. Expecting a budget Mac to do everything. The MacBook Neo suits everyday coursework, but its multi-core performance lags, so heavy video or similar work will feel slower.
