4 mistakes people make when buying a perfume

  1. Buying before you smell it on skin. PureWow suggests trying samples in store, and Marie Claire UK says to start with a blotter, then spray your wrist. Scents like Glossier You smell different on each person.
  2. Rubbing your wrists together. Marie Claire UK calls this a cardinal sin if you want the scent to last longer.
  3. Ignoring the season. PureWow flags Clinique Happy as possibly too floral for fall and winter, while warmer scents like Cheirosa 62 suit cold evenings better.
  4. Expecting every perfume to last all day. Wear time varies: PureWow reports about 5+ hours for Glossier You on skin, about six hours for Cheirosa 62 and 14+ hours for Athénaïs.

FAQ

What is the best perfume for most people?

Glossier You is the safest first pick. Marie Claire UK and PureWow both list it, and it is a mild skin scent. It lasts about 5+ hours on skin.

Which perfume lasts the longest?

Parfums de Marly Athénaïs. PureWow's editor reports 14+ hours of wear. It has a high price point and a sweetness that borders on coy.

What is a good evening perfume?

Diptyque Orphéon. Both Marie Claire UK and PureWow praise it as a deep, woody scent. It comes in one 2.4 oz size only.

What is the difference between eau de parfum and eau de toilette?

Marie Claire UK says eau de parfum has 15-20% perfume oil and lasts about six to eight hours, while eau de toilette has 5-15% and lasts about two to four.

Is there a fresh perfume for summer?

Clinique Happy, a bright citrus floral with grapefruit and bergamot. PureWow says it may be too floral for fall and winter.
