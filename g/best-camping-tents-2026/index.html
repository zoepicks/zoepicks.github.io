How to choose

  1. Size up. Outdoor Life says a 4-person tent only just fits four sleeping pads, so choose a 6-person tent if you need real space for four people.
  2. Check peak height. CleverHiker says it matters most if you will spend time awake inside. Tall tents catch more wind, while low tents are steadier and lighter.
  3. Look for a full-coverage fly in rain. CleverHiker says full flies protect better in long rain while partial flies improve warm-weather airflow.
  4. Budget for stakes and a footprint. Several tents here do not include enough stakes or a footprint, so add what is missing before the trip.
