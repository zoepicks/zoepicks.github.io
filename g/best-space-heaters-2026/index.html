How to choose

  1. Size it to the room. Bob Vila says about 10 watts per square foot, so a 1,500W heater suits roughly a 15 x 10 ft room. HGTV found none of the big-room claims held up, so buy a bit stronger than you need and turn it down.
  2. Prioritize safety features. Look for tip-over and overheat protection; use ALCI plugs in damp rooms and keep 3 feet of clearance from fabric and furniture (HGTV, Bob Vila).
  3. Never use an extension cord. Bob Vila warns this raises fire risk; plug directly into the wall.
  4. Use it as supplemental heat. HGTV says space heaters are not meant to replace central heating and will raise your bill if used that way.

FAQ

Can I leave a space heater on overnight?

HGTV says most makers and the CPSC advise against it because of fire risk; warm the room first and turn it off before bed.

Ceramic or infrared?

Infrared costs more but is quieter and more efficient, while ceramic is cheaper, louder and can dry the air (HGTV).

Where should I put it?

On a level, hard, non-flammable surface at least 3 feet from anything combustible, away from foot traffic (HGTV).
