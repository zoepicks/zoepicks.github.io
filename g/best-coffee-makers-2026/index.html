4 mistakes that ruin good coffee

  1. Buying a bigger pot than you drink. Coffee loses flavor on a hot plate. Match the carafe to your household, or choose a thermal carafe.
  2. Using old beans. Fresh beans matter more than the machine. Buy whole beans and use them within a few weeks of roasting.
  3. Never descaling. Mineral buildup lowers the water temperature and slows the brew. Descale every month or two.
  4. Ignoring the SCA seal. It means the machine meets set standards for brew temperature and time, so it is the quickest quality check on the box.

FAQ

What is the best coffee maker for most people?

The OXO Brew 9-Cup. It is SCA-certified, has a thermal carafe and a timer, and costs far less than the Moccamaster.

Is the Moccamaster worth the money?

If you drink drip coffee daily, yes. It is built to last and brews at the right temperature every time. If you drink a cup now and then, a cheaper pick is smarter.

Glass or thermal carafe?

Thermal keeps coffee hot without a hot plate, so it does not taste cooked later. Glass is fine if you finish the pot quickly.

Do I need cold brew too?

Only if you drink iced coffee regularly. The OXO cold brew maker makes a concentrate that keeps in the fridge for up to two weeks.
