How to choose

  1. Match loft to your position. Tom's Guide says side sleepers need a high loft to fill the shoulder gap, while back and stomach sleepers do fine with lower loft.
  2. Match firmness too. Soft suits stomach sleepers, firm helps side sleepers keep neck and back aligned, and medium-firm works for back and combo sleepers.
  3. Pick the right fill. Memory foam contours but can run warm, latex is naturally cooler, and allergy sufferers should skip feathers for a down alternative.
  4. Get a trial. TechRadar recommends at least three weeks to judge a pillow; Tom's Guide says to replace pillows every one to two years.
