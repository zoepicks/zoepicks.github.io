How to choose

  1. Centrifugal or masticating. Tom's Guide says centrifugal juicers are faster, cheaper and noisier, while slow or cold press juicers are quieter, extract more and preserve fiber.
  2. Greens need a slow juicer. Tom's Guide says a masticating juicer is better for spinach, kale and celery, while centrifugal models blitz through hard apples and carrots.
  3. Mind the chute. BestReviews notes a wide chute cuts chopping, but a compact machine may fit your counter better.
  4. Plan for cleanup. Most cold press parts need hand rinsing. BestReviews advises washing or soaking right after use so pulp does not dry.
