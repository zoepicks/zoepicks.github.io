5 mistakes that put a juicer in the cupboard

  1. Buying the wrong type for your produce. For spinach, kale and celery, choose a cold-press (masticating) juicer. Centrifugal ones often throw light leaves into the pulp bin.
  2. Ignoring noise. Tom's Guide says centrifugal juicers can sound like a loud blender, while slow juicers measured as low as 60 dB. Check this if you juice early in the morning.
  3. Not thinking about cleanup. If it takes ten minutes to clean, you will stop using it. Rinse the parts right after juicing, because pulp dries hard.
  4. Skipping the warranty. The Nama J3 has a 15-year warranty, while the AMZCHEF has none. On a juicer you plan to use often, that matters.
  5. Expecting the juice to keep. Centrifugal juice should be drunk right away. Cold-press juice keeps in the fridge for up to 72 hours, according to Tom's Guide.
