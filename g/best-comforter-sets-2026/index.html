5 mistakes that ruin a good night under a comforter

  1. Buying the wrong warmth for your room. Pick the weight for your bedroom temperature and how you sleep. Hot sleepers should look at all-season or lightweight, and cold sleepers at heavyweight.
  2. Ignoring allergies. Down and feathers can trigger allergies. A down alternative, like the Layla or Saatva, is the safer choice.
  3. Forgetting how you will wash it. Brooklinen's down only fits commercial washers, and Cozy Earth's bamboo is not machine washable. Layla and Saatva can go in a home machine.
  4. Skipping the trial period. Trials range from 30 nights (Layla) to 365 nights (Brooklinen). A long trial lets you test a comforter through changing seasons.
  5. Expecting a full bedding set. These are comforters and inserts. Sheets and a duvet cover are sold separately.
