Canon EOS R50

Why we picked it

Tom's Guide and Digital Camera World both pick it as the best camera for beginners. They praise the image quality from its 24.2MP APS-C sensor, the strong autofocus and an easy-to-use menu, and Digital Camera World says the autofocus is what seals it.

It has Dual Pixel CMOS AF II with people, animal and vehicle detection, a fully articulating touchscreen and an electronic viewfinder. Fstoppers adds that it has one of the best guided menus for learning exposure, but notes Canon has sold through its own stock and a refreshed model is expected.

Key specs
  • 24.2MP APS-C sensor, DIGIC X processor
  • Dual Pixel CMOS AF II with subject detection
  • Fully articulating touchscreen and electronic viewfinder
  • 13.2 oz / 375 g, Canon RF mount, no in-body stabilization
Pros
  • Excellent autofocus for the price
  • Beginner-friendly menus and guidance
  • Small and light
Cons
  • No in-body image stabilization
  • Stock may get scarce as Canon clears it out
Skip it if: You want a camera you will not outgrow for years

Tom's Guide's best budget pick and Fstoppers' best budget pick. It is Canon's simplest entry into the RF mirrorless system, and Tom's Guide says the extra spend on the R50 is worth it if you can afford it.

  • 12.6 oz / 356 g, Canon RF mount

  • Lowest cost of the group for a new mirrorless kit

  • Same RF lens mount as the R50
Cons
  • Very basic compared with the R50
  • Fixed non-touch screen
  • Weaker autofocus than the R50

Why we picked it

TechRadar calls it a great camera to improve with: its Expeed 7 processor gives flagship-level subject detection, it feels good in the hand, and it takes good stills and video straight out of the box. Fstoppers ranks it best overall for beginners but says it has not reviewed it in house.

It works with Nikon's full-frame Z lenses, so you can step up later. It costs and weighs more than the Canon R50 and has a short rated battery life.

Key specs
  • 20.9MP APS-C sensor, Expeed 7 processor
  • 3D-tracking autofocus with subject detection
  • 17.5 oz / 495 g, Nikon Z mount
  • Works with Nikon's full-frame Z lenses
Pros
  • Flagship-grade subject-detection autofocus
  • Room to grow into a Nikon Z system
  • Comfortable ergonomics
Cons
  • More expensive and heavier than the R50
  • Short rated battery life
  • Resolution beaten by some rivals
Skip it if: You want the smallest, cheapest kit
