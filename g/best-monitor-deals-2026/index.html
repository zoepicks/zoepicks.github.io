How to spot a real monitor deal

  1. Compare with the lowest recent price, not the crossed-out list price. A big percentage off an inflated list price is not always a bargain.
  2. Match the exact model number. Monitor names are close to each other, so check the full model before you buy, as with the Dell S2725QS above.
  3. Pick resolution before refresh rate. A sharp 1440p or 4K panel is a bigger everyday upgrade than a very high refresh rate, unless you play competitive games.
  4. Check the ports. If you want one cable to charge a laptop, you need USB-C with power delivery. Not every monitor has it.
  5. Check the return window before you buy, since a monitor is easier to judge once it is on your own desk.

FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30.

Should I buy now or wait for Black Friday?

Some monitor prices in this guide were already low in October's Prime sale. If a monitor you want is at a price you are happy with, buying now is reasonable. Prices can also drop further closer to Black Friday, so check again before the sale.

Are 4K monitors worth it?

For work, browsing and streaming, 4K text looks noticeably sharper. For gaming, a 4K screen needs a stronger PC than 1440p, so 1440p is the easier choice if you are on a budget.

Is OLED worth paying extra for?

OLED screens give deeper blacks and very fast response, which gamers and movie fans like. They cost more than most LCD monitors, so decide if the picture quality is worth it for you.

Is the Amazon price the same as Best Buy?

Not always. The LG UltraGear G6 deal was reported at Best Buy, and the Odyssey G50SF was higher at Best Buy than Amazon. Use the Amazon button to compare the current price and exact model.
