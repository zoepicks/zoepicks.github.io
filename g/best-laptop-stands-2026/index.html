4 mistakes people make when buying a laptop stand

  1. Skipping the external keyboard. A stand lifts the screen to eye level, so you type on a separate keyboard at desk height. The Gadgeteer notes that with the laptop raised, typing on its own keyboard makes your wrists ache.
  2. Ignoring wobble. Any flex at the top shows up as screen wobble every time you type. The Gadgeteer says reviews that mention bouncy typing are a red flag.
  3. Not checking the size range. The Lamicall fits 10 to 17-inch laptops and the Roost fits 12- to 18-inch ones. Check your laptop against the range before you buy.
  4. Expecting a stand to fix cooling. The gap under the laptop helps airflow, but The Gadgeteer says a stand will not rescue a laptop with a failing fan.
