4 mistakes when buying a budget laptop

  1. Paying the first price you see. PCWorld saw the Nitro V 16 AI far higher on Amazon than at other stores, and the Swift 16 AI just under the budget line at only one store.
  2. Buying a weak configuration. iTechGuides warns that 4GB or 128GB models of the IdeaPad Slim 3 are weak for daily use. For the Vivobook 16, look for 16GB of RAM and a 512GB SSD.
  3. Forgetting that a Chromebook runs ChromeOS only. The Chromebook picks cannot install Windows or macOS desktop software, so check your classes or work first.
  4. Ignoring Windows on Arm. The IdeaPad Slim 3x has an ARM chip, so check that your apps support Windows on ARM.
