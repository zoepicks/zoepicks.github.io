How to choose

  1. Budget $200 to $350. NBC Select's expert says a quality beginner machine costs about that much and that a $79 machine tends to jam and cannot handle varied fabric thickness.
  2. Skip the bells and whistles. The same expert says beginners mostly need a great straight stitch and zigzag, and warns that heavily computerized machines cost more to fix.
  3. Match the machine to your fabric. BestReviews says thick denim and canvas call for a strong motor, a metal frame and a high presser foot lift; lighter machines can jam on heavy layers.
  4. Look for convenience features. BestReviews recommends a drop-in bobbin, automatic needle threader and one-step buttonhole to cut down on frustration.

FAQ

Mechanical or computerized?

Mechanical machines use dials and are simpler and cheaper to maintain; computerized ones offer more stitches and embroidery but can cost more to repair (BestReviews).

Should I buy a mini sewing machine?

NBC Select's experts advise passing on minis, which are only good for tiny repairs.

How often should I service it?

BestReviews says at least once every two years, or more often if you sew frequently, and to replace needles after about 16 hours of use.
