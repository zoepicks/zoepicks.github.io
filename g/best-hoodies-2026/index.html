How to choose

  1. Check the fabric on the tag. Gear Patrol says the material listed on the tag, such as French terry or fleece-back, does more than anything else to decide a hoodie's look, feel and longevity.
  2. Match the weight to the weather. Men's Health says lightweight hoodies suit indoors and heavyweight ones suit outdoors, and it warns that Champion's thick build may be too hot for summer.
  3. Pick the fit you will actually wear. Men's Health lists regular, slim and relaxed fits. Gear Patrol says to size up on the slim Reigning Champ if you like a boxy cut, and that Flint and Tinder runs small for many users.
  4. Look for shrink protection. Both guides credit Champion's reverse weave with reducing shrinkage, which matters if you wash your hoodie often.
