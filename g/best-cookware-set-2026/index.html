5 things to check before buying cookware

  1. Look for 'fully clad'. That means the aluminum core runs up the sides, not just in the base, so the pan heats evenly.
  2. Check induction. Never assume a set works on induction. Look for an induction-ready label.
  3. Choose stainless or nonstick on purpose. Stainless lasts longest and takes a little technique. Ceramic nonstick is easier but wears sooner.
  4. Count what you will use. A 12-piece set often includes lids and small pans. Make sure the pieces you cook with every day are in the box.
  5. Protect the finish. Hand-wash premium stainless to keep it looking new, and use low to medium heat on ceramic nonstick.
