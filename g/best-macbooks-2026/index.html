4 mistakes when buying a MacBook

  1. Treating the Neo like an Air. Refurbed says the M5 in the Air has roughly double the multi-core and GPU performance of the Neo's A18 Pro chip.
  2. Buying too little memory or storage on an Air. The Air's RAM and storage are fixed, so you cannot upgrade them later.
  3. Buying a Pro for light work. If you mostly browse, write and stream, the Pro is more than you need.
  4. Expecting a big jump from the 14-inch M5. Tom's Guide notes the 14-inch M5 is not a huge upgrade over the M4 model.
