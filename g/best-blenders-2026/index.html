How to avoid buying a blender twice

  1. Decide what you blend most. Smoothies and ice need power. Soups need heat or a soup preset. Occasional use needs less money.
  2. Think about counter space. Tall high-power containers can be hard to fit under cabinets.
  3. Count the warranty. A long warranty is part of why premium blenders are often called buy-it-once appliances.
  4. Expect noise. High-power blenders are loud. Run them for short bursts and not at night.

FAQ

Which blender is best for most people?

The Vitamix Ascent X5 for the best all-round results. The Ninja TWISTi is the one to buy if you are on a budget.

Is a Vitamix worth it?

If you blend most days, yes. It is built to last and handles ice and hot soup well. If you blend a few times a month, a cheaper blender is enough.

Blender or hand blender?

A blender is better for smoothies and ice. A hand blender is better for soup in the pot and quick cleanup.
