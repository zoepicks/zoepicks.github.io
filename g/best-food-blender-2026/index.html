Before you buy a hand blender

  1. Check what it can't do. Hand blenders are for soup, sauces and light prep. None of the picks here crushes ice well, so for frozen drinks buy a countertop blender.
  2. Pick the right size. The long arm of the Breville reaches into a deep stockpot. A compact one is easier to store.
  3. Choose cord or cordless. A cordless model like the KitchenAid Go moves freely but has less sustained power for big batches.
  4. Protect your pans. Tilt the blender so the blade never scrapes the bottom, and use the blade guard that comes with most models on coated or delicate pans.

FAQ

What is the best immersion blender?

The Breville Control Grip is our overall pick. The Braun MultiQuick 7 is the best if you want the most attachments.

Can an immersion blender crush ice?

Most cannot do it well. RTINGS rates even the Breville only mediocre at crushing ice.

Cordless or corded?

Cordless is easier to move around the kitchen, corded gives steady power for large batches of soup.

Immersion blender or countertop blender?

Hand blender for soups, sauces and quick cleanup. Countertop blender for smoothies, ice and big batches.
