How to choose

  1. Choose a size. Full-size boards have a number pad, tenkeyless drops it, and a 60% layout keeps only the main keys. Tom's Guide says to pick by desk space and how you use it.
  2. Linear or tactile. Linear switches press smoothly all the way, while tactile switches register about halfway down with a bump. Tom's Guide says to decide on quiet versus loud first.
  3. Look for hot-swappable switches. Boards like the Keychron V1 Ultra 8K and V6 Max let you swap switches without soldering, which extends their life.
  4. Wired or wireless. The V1 8K is wired only and the V1 Ultra 8K is wireless. Tom's Guide notes many people only need 1,000Hz polling.
