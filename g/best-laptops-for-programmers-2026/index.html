4 mistakes when buying a laptop for coding

  1. Buying 8GB of RAM. Sources agree 16GB is the sensible minimum for programming. 8GB only suits basic learning.
  2. Choosing macOS when you need Windows-only tools. The MacBook Pro and MacBook Air both run macOS. If your tools are Windows-only, look at the ThinkPad T16.
  3. Expecting a thin laptop to handle heavy builds. The MacBook Air has less headroom than the MacBook Pro for heavy workloads, including big builds and local AI models.
  4. Forgetting Linux support. TechRadar says ThinkPads run Linux very well. If you work on Linux, that matters more than the thinnest design.
