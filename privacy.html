Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit zoepicks.github.io (the “Site”), how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the Site you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not require accounts, and the Site has no sign-up or comment forms. We do not ask you for personal information. The following information may be collected automatically:

  • Hosting logs. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, under GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Anonymous visit counter. To show how many people are on the Site, a small script stores a random identifier in your browser’s local storage and sends it, with a timestamp, to our own server. The identifier is not linked to your name or contact details.
  • Email. If you email us, we keep your message and address only to respond to you.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. The Site itself uses local storage only for the anonymous visit identifier described above. Third parties whose content or links appear on the Site (see below) may set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the Site will still work.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-party services
